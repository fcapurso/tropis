**Page length:** at most 1,200 words, counted as `wc -w` counts them, title and fixed text included.

## What it is
- **Job:** one paragraph a newcomer can read in ten seconds: what this is and what problem it solves.
- **Facts from:** the project brief (Problem, Solution), the shipped backlog items.
- **Length:** one paragraph.

## What it does for you
- **Job:** an overview of what someone gets, across a fixed set of five to seven aspects of the work, one bullet per aspect, each saying in a sentence or two what they get there. No bullet describes a single feature: a feature that ships joins the aspect it belongs to, in that bullet's existing words where they still hold, and its detail is left to the documents the page links to.
- **Facts from:** the shipped backlog items of layer product, type feature or improvement.
- **Length:** five to seven bullets.

## Who it is for
- **Job:** who it fits and who it does not, so a reader can rule themselves in or out. What it needs to work goes under What it needs, not here.
- **Facts from:** the project brief, the decision record.
- **Length:** two short paragraphs.

## What it needs
- **Job:** what someone must have for it to work, at a high level: tools, accounts, services, platforms. Each is marked required or optional, with what is lost without an optional one. This is the only section that lists them; Getting started shows how to set them up.
- **Facts from:** the setup scripts and package files, the CI configuration, the architecture document, the project brief.
- **Length:** a short list.

## Getting started
- **Job:** the shortest path to a first success, as numbered steps that work as written.
- **Facts from:** the setup scripts and commands, the operator guide.
- **Length:** as few steps as it takes.

## How it works
- **Job:** an overview of how the parts fit, linking to the deeper documents instead of copying them.
- **Facts from:** the architecture document, the decision record, the framework documents.
- **Length:** a few paragraphs.

## Using it day to day
- **Job:** what someone does once it is set up, around the part they play: what they decide, do and check, and when, naming the command or screen that serves each. What the product does for them on its own is said as such, not listed as steps of theirs. Not a list of every command.
- **Facts from:** the installed commands and scripts, the operator guide, the project brief.
- **Length:** a short section.

## Where it stands
- **Job:** what works today and the known limits, honestly. A requirement goes under What it needs; a limit here is something not supported yet.
- **Facts from:** the backlog (shipped, building, shelved), the release notes.
- **Length:** a short list.

## Where to go next
- **Job:** links to the deeper documents, how to contribute, the licence.
- **Facts from:** the repository's own documents.
- **Length:** a short list.
