Bands. A do now — high value, and urgent or blocking other work. B do next — worth doing, no deadline. C decide first — an open question only the developer can answer stands between the entry and a plan. D leave it — nothing to do until something happens. Inside a band, entries are ranked by value divided by effort, each scored 1 to 5 in the bands file with a reason; an entry without both scores comes last. An entry waiting on another is banded on its own value and marked with what it waits on. Shipped, replaced and shelved entries are not banded: they are listed under Closed. Claims marked checked against the code were verified against the source, not taken from the entry text.

Arcs are derived the way npm run next derives them — entries sharing a design spec, ordered by after. The board then goes one step further: a superseded entry is left out of an arc's count, since its work moved to another entry, so an arc whose only remaining step is superseded is shown as done rather than in progress. next does not yet make that distinction and reports such arcs as open. Group by Arc to see each design's full sequence: shipped steps in green, open ones hollow, parked or superseded ones struck through.
